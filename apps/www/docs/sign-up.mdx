## Step 1: Start the Sign-Up Process

1. Visit the website at [www.squaredmade.com](https://www.squaredmade.com).
2. On the landing screen, click **Sign Up**. You’ll be taken to the registration page where you can choose how you want to sign up (e.g., **Email**, **Google**).

## Step 2: Choose Your Registration Method

Squared offers several ways to create an account:
- **Email**: Register with a valid email address and create a unique password.
- **Google**: Sign up quickly using your Google account.

## Step 3: Enter Your Information

- **For Email Sign-Up**: Enter your email address and create a secure password.  
    > **Tip**: Your password should be at least **8 characters** long and include **letters, numbers, special characters and one capital letter**.
- **For Social Sign-Up**: Allow Squared to access information from your Google account to complete registration.

## Step 4: Verify Your Email Address

If you signed up with an email, check your inbox for a verification email from Squared.

1. Open the email and click on the **Confirm Email** link to complete the process.
    > **Tip**: If you don’t see the email, check your spam or junk folder. You can also request a new email from the sign-up page.

## Step 5: Start Using Squared

Congratulations! Your Squared account is now active. Log in anytime with your email and password or social account.
