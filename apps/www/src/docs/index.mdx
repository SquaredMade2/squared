# Welcome to Squared Documentation

Squared is a powerful web-based workflow management system designed to help teams organize their projects efficiently. Our platform provides a comprehensive set of tools to streamline your development process, enhance collaboration, and boost productivity.

## Key Features

1. **Task Tracking and Evolution**
   - Keep a detailed history of your tasks
   - Visualize progress over time
   - Easily prioritize and manage your workload

2. **Team Notifications**
   - Receive automatic updates on team activities
   - Stay informed with customizable email notifications
   - Never miss important project developments

3. **Intuitive Task Tracking Interface**
   - User-friendly task creation and assignment
   - Effortless team collaboration
   - Streamlined workflow management

4. **Extensive Integrations**
   - Seamless connection with popular git clients
   - Slack integration for real-time team communication
   - Bridge the gap between engineering and design teams
